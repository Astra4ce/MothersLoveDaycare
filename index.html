Our Mission

For over 30 years, Mother's Love Preschool & Daycare has been a trusted partner for families in Borough Park, Brooklyn, providing a safe, nurturing, and inspiring environment where every child feels valued, confident, and excited to learn.

Our experienced, New York City Department of Education licensed teachers create a healthy balance of child-directed exploration and teacher-guided instruction, allowing children to learn independently, collaborate with their peers, and grow through purposeful play and discovery.

As a bilingual program, we proudly support English language development while honoring and respecting each child's home language and culture — creating an environment where every child and family feels welcomed, respected, and valued.

We believe parents are a child's first teachers. By building strong partnerships with families through open communication, we work together to support each child's growth, confidence, and success both in school and at home.

Nutrition

Fresh, home-style meals, every day

We provide fresh, home-style meals that are professionally catered and delivered to our school daily. Children enjoy a nutritious breakfast, lunch, and afternoon snack each day. Our lunches include soup, a chicken/fish entrée, a wholesome side dish, and a fresh salad. Milk is served with every meal, and fresh drinking water is always available throughout the day.

We are committed to providing healthy, balanced meals that help children feel nourished, energized, and right at home.
